# Org Structure — 20 people

The reference company we are modelling. Numbers in parentheses are headcount per function.

## Leadership (2)
- Founder / CEO
- COO (operations head)

## R&D (3)
Works on next-generation products and continuous improvement of the current AQI monitor.
- Hardware R&D lead (PCB, sensors, mechanical)
- Firmware / embedded engineer
- Industrial designer (CAD, enclosures) — may be part-time

## Manufacturing & QC (5)
In-house assembly of the AQI monitor.
- Production lead
- Assembly operators × 3
- QC inspector (incoming, in-process, final + calibration)

## Supply Chain (2)
- Procurement (electronic components + white-label speaker sourcing + China imports)
- Warehouse & dispatch (Shiprocket → Delhivery / Blue Dart)

## Sales (3)
- D2C lead (Shopify + Amazon India)
- B2B sales (corporates, schools, hospitals)
- Inside sales / lead qualification

## Marketing (2)
- Content & social (Instagram, YouTube)
- Performance marketing (Meta, Google ads)

## Support (1)
- RMA, warranty, WhatsApp Business tickets

## Finance & HR (2)
- Accounts (Tally / Zoho Books, GST, reconciliation)
- HR / admin (attendance, payroll with PF/ESI, hiring, statutory)

**Total: 20**

## Cross-functional notes

- Most functions are **single-person owners** — the tracker for that function is their daily work surface.
- R&D ↔ Manufacturing handoff is the most workflow-heavy interface (BOM freeze, pilot batches, defect feedback loop).
- The founder reviews **across all functions** daily — this is precisely where Claude Code adds the most leverage.
