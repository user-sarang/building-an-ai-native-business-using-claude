# Samalyal — Brand Profile

> The creator and reviewer agents both read this file. It is the single source of
> truth for what "on-brand" means for Samalyal. Keep it current.

## One-line

**Samalyal** is a warm, home-cooked, mom-and-pop food brand — the taste and feeling
of a mother's kitchen, made with love and served like family.

## Positioning

Where the big delivery brands (Swiggy, Big Bowl, etc.) shout about price and pile on
loud, photo-real "food porn," Samalyal does the opposite: it feels **homely, honest,
and hand-made**. The promise is emotional warmth and authenticity, not a discount.

- **Category:** home-cooked / comfort food, mom-and-pop kitchen.
- **Feeling:** warmth, nostalgia, care, belonging, "ghar ka khana."
- **Hero is the feeling**, not a screaming price tag.

## Color palette

Purple is the signature. Anchor every creative in it.

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|------|-------|-----|-----|
| Primary | Samalyal Purple | `#6B3FA0` | Backgrounds, logo, headlines |
| Deep | Aubergine | `#3D2259` | Depth, shadows, text on light |
| Light | Lavender Cream | `#EDE6F5` | Soft backgrounds, breathing space |
| Warm accent | Turmeric Gold | `#E8A33D` | CTAs, highlights, warmth |
| Warm accent 2 | Terracotta | `#C8623F` | Secondary accent, earthy warmth |
| Neutral | Warm Cream | `#FBF6EE` | Paper / canvas feel |

Rule: **purple leads, warm gold/terracotta supports.** Never let the warm accents
take over — they are seasoning, not the dish.

## Typography direction

- **Headlines:** a friendly, slightly rounded humanist serif or a warm hand-lettered
  style — feels personal and homemade, never corporate.
- **Body / labels:** a clean, readable humanist sans.
- **Tone of type:** cozy, legible, generous spacing. No aggressive italic price slabs.

## Illustration & art style

This is the heart of the brand. Samalyal creatives are **illustration-led**, not
stock food photography.

- Warm, hand-drawn / painterly illustration. Visible texture, soft edges.
- Cozy domestic scenes: a home kitchen, steam rising from a bowl, hands serving,
  a family table, grandmother's utensils.
- Soft, warm lighting — golden-hour kitchen glow.
- Purple-dominant palette with warm gold/terracotta highlights.
- Inviting negative space (lavender cream / warm cream).
- Generous, friendly composition. Feels like a storybook, not a billboard.

## Voice & copy

- Warm, intimate, a little nostalgic. Speaks like family.
- Short, heartfelt lines over punchy sales slogans.
- Examples of tone: "Made the way Maa makes it." · "A little love in every bowl." ·
  "Home, served warm."
- Hinglish is welcome when it feels affectionate, never gimmicky.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Lead with purple; keep warmth and softness.
- Use illustration, texture, hand-made feel.
- Center the emotion of home-cooked care.
- Keep one clear, gentle message per creative.

**Don't**

- Don't scream prices or use big slashed-price slabs (that's the competitor lane).
- Don't use harsh high-contrast orange/red/yellow delivery-app styling.
- Don't use cold, glossy, hyper-real stock food photography.
- Don't clutter — no busy stickers, no neon, no aggressive arrows.

## Differentiation vs competitors (from inputs/competitor)

Observed competitor pattern (Swiggy, Big Bowl): 9:16 format, loud orange/yellow/red,
giant ₹ price callouts, glossy food hero shots, punchy Hinglish slogans
("Hunger Ka Superhero", "Say Cheese at ₹99"), hard "ORDER NOW" CTAs.

Samalyal wins by being the **calm, warm, human alternative**: purple, illustrated,
emotional, homely. If a creative could be mistaken for a Swiggy ad, it has failed.

## Required usage rules (audited)

1. Purple is visibly the dominant color.
2. No price-slab / discount-shout styling.
3. Illustration or hand-made look — not photoreal stock.
4. Warm, homely emotional tone.
5. Logo (if present) undistorted with clear space.
6. Copy spelled correctly and legible.
