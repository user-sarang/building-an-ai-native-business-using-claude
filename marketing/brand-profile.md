- **Product lines:** Core (and only) line — the desk AQI + clock monitor, ₹2,000–₹5,000. Maps to the catalog SKU `AQM-PRO-01` ("AirSense Pro", ₹4,999). No secondary lines yet.

## 2. Audience & positioning
- **Primary segments:** B2C — urban families / parents who are health-conscious about the air at home.
- **Jobs-to-be-done / pains:** A functional desk upgrade — a good-looking clock that also surfaces air quality, so the desk gets something useful and ambient rather than a clinical "instrument."
- **Competitors:** Airveda, Prana Air, Atmotube (category default — confirm the real head-to-heads).
- **Differentiator:** Better product quality — specifically the **display** and the **battery life**.
- **The belief we want to leave:** "A nicer desk piece that just happens to keep an honest eye on your air — and lasts."

## 4. Voice & writing style
- **Tone adjectives:** Clear, no-nonsense, plain, factual.
- **Reading level:** Simple & everyday (~8th grade), conversational.
- **We say (love-words/phrases):** Concrete claims about the **display** and **battery life**; plain numbers ("8 days on a charge", "reads PM2.5"); "at a glance"; "on your desk".
- **We never say (ban-words/phrases):** Hype words — "revolutionary", "game-changer", "world's best", "cutting-edge", fear-mongering health scare language. No exclamation-heavy copy.
- **Emoji policy:** None.
- **Sample we're proud of:** `TBD` — add a line of copy you like and it becomes the reference.
- **Do / Don't:**
  - Do: "A clear display you can read across the room, and a battery that lasts days."
  - Don't: "The revolutionary AI-powered air guardian that will change your life! 🌬️"

## 7. Campaign & UTM conventions
- **How we think about campaigns:** Always-on (steady ads running continuously, tuned over time), with room for seasonal bursts later.
- **Typical length:** Open-ended for always-on; bursts run a few weeks.
- **utm_campaign scheme:** `{theme}-{objective}-{year}`, lowercase-hyphenated — e.g. `always-on-conversions-2026`, `desk-upgrade-awareness-2026`, `pollution-season-2026`. This exact string is the join key across ad platforms, creatives, and sales coupon/UTM.
- **Objectives we run:** Awareness, Traffic, Conversions (primary), Retention (later).
